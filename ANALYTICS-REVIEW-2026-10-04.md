## What the data says

1. **Answering followers directly is the only thing that has produced sales.** Two requests, two articles,
   two orders on the same day. Do more of this: ask followers what they're shopping for.
2. **The audience is the user's existing followers** (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok lives), not strangers from
   search or Pinterest. Content that reaches them converts; cold channels haven't.
3. **Pinterest has one live niche: pendulum.** Double down there; stop adding generic organizing pins until
   something on Picks gets a click.
4. **X is not worth the effort** at 2 followers. Keep it to the minimum.
5. **Search needs months**, and the brand-name-only queries show the articles aren't ranking for anything yet.
