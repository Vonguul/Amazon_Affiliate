## Suggested article angle

"Indoor Growing Systems: What to Buy Depending on How Much You Want to Grow". The three tiers (countertop
garden → shelf with grow light → grow tent), then the supplies each tier actually needs. Framed for herbs,
salad greens and vegetables. Honest notes: countertop gardens lock you into their pods (Click & Grow);
hydroponic systems need pH and nutrient upkeep; grow lights need a timer; AeroGarden's 2024-25 shutdown and
relaunch.
