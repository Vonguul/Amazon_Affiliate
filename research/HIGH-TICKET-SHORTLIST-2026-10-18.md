| 2.5% | PC and components |
| 2% | Televisions |
| 1% | Grocery, Health & Personal Care, video game consoles |

CAVEAT: a May 2026 report (Shopifreaks citing Adweek, not confirmed by Amazon) says Amazon quietly
cut some rates from 10% to 4-5% in the US from about March 2026 and removed bonus tiers. The page
above does not show that, so treat rates as upper bounds and check Associates Central.
The category an ASIN falls in (not the article topic) decides its rate, so check per product.

Expected commission per sale = price x rate. A 4.5% kitchen item at a high price beats a 3% home
item at a similar price. Clicking through and buying anything in the 24h window also pays.
