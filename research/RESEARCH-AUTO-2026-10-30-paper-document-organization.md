## Writing notes

- Angle: every paper is one of three things: act on it, keep it, or destroy it. Each gets one home.
- Order: desk sorter for what's incoming, hanging files for what's active, archive boxes for old
  years, shredder for what's left. Fireproof bag for what can't be replaced.
- Fire ratings are maker claims, and a fire bag is not a safe. Say so in "Worth being direct about",
  check the listing wording when building, and never promise protection.
- Don't give tax or legal advice on how long to keep records: point to the IRS guidance and say to ask a
  tax professional.
- Links: home-office-organization-ideas, digital-privacy-security-essentials (shredding fits here).
- No prices or "under $X" framing anywhere.
