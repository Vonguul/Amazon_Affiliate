# PROPOSAL 2026-11-05 — Winter power outage prep: lighting, power and a kit you can find in the dark

**Site:** Vonguul Picks
**Status:** proposed, waiting for the owner's product check (written by the daily routine; no products were checked on Amazon)

## Angle

Most homes have outage supplies, just not in one place: a flashlight with dead batteries in one drawer, candles in another, phone
chargers that need the wall. The article organizes outage prep the way the rest of the site organizes rooms: one bin, one shelf,
everything reachable in the dark, plus the few items that actually keep a room lit and phones charged for a night.

**Why now:** winter storms and ice cause outages from December to February, and people shop for this before the first one, not
after. Pairs with the existing winter articles.

## Quality bar

- In stock, sold by Amazon or a reputable seller (the brand's own store counts).
- Rating about 4.3 or better with a real review count (hundreds, not a handful).
- ASIN not already on either site (ask for a duplicate check in a session; the junk drawer page already has a battery organizer and
  the gift guide and car trunk pages have a car emergency kit).
- For the power station and power bank: a known brand, and capacity stated in the listing.

## Writing notes

- Structure like the other organization pages: one problem per section (light a room, free your hands, keep phones charged,
  hear alerts, keep it all in one place, water).
- No prices. No safety claims beyond what a listing states. Do not suggest running generators indoors or using candles as the main
  light; keep fuel-burning devices out of the article entirely.
- A short "where to keep the bin" section: somewhere you can reach in the dark, not the back of the garage.
- Mention checking and recharging the kit when clocks change, as a habit, not a product.
