Writing notes: no health claims ("symptom relief" is in some Levoit titles, don't repeat it).
Keep indoor humidity around 30 to 50% (EPA guidance), which is why the hygrometer belongs in the
article; clean the tank regularly per the manual; ultrasonic units with hard tap water can leave
white mineral dust (use distilled water, or choose evaporative); warm-mist units have a hot
reservoir, keep away from kids and pets. Size by tank and output, and treat "up to X sq ft"
claims loosely: there's no AHAM-style standard like CADR for humidifier coverage.
