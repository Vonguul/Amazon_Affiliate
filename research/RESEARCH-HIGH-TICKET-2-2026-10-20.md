# Picks high-ticket research, round 2 (2026-10-20): air purifiers, mattresses, bedding

Method: amazon.com search, then product pages read live (rating, reviews, stock, seller,
category). None of these ASINs is used on either site. Prices are internal notes only.
Independent testing: RTINGS mattress page (updated Aug 26, 2026) was readable; RTINGS, Wirecutter
and Consumer Reports air purifier pages were paywalled or unreachable, so purifier picks lean on
the 2026-10-18 shortlist notes (RTINGS best: Winix 5530; CR extra-large: Coway Airmega ProX) plus
Amazon evidence. Do not claim "lab tested" for anything not named by a tester.

Commission (published table, subject to the reported 2026 cuts): air purifiers and mattresses are
Home/Furniture, likely 3%.
