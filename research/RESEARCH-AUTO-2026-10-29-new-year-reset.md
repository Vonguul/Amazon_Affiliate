## Writing notes

- Angle: "one weekend, one system": label first (so the system survives), then the closet, then
  clear bins for everything that lives out of sight. Order matters: declutter before buying bins.
- Closet kits are big-ticket; say to measure the closet width first and match the kit range
  (2-4 ft vs 3-6 ft). Mention ShelfTrack mounts to the wall, Configurations is mostly freestanding
  with top mounting (verify against the listing when building).
- Clear bins: same size and brand so lids stay interchangeable; label the short end.
- Links: home-office-organization-ideas (label maker), small-bedroom-closet-organization-ideas,
  garage-organization-ideas, holiday-seasonal-decor-storage-ideas.
- No prices or "under $X" framing anywhere.
