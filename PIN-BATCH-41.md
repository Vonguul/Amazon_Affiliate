**Title:** Wet Boots by the Door Are Still Wet in the Morning. Fix That.

**Description:** A boot tray protects the floor, but boots stay damp inside. A forced-air dryer dries two pairs overnight, gloves too. Plus where every hat, glove and scarf should live so the entry stops being a pile. #wintergear #entrywayorganization #bootdryer

**Title:** One Pocket Per Person: The End of the Missing Glove

**Description:** Hats and gloves get lost because they go into one shared pile. A mesh pocket organizer on the back of the door gives each person a row, so a missing glove has exactly one place to be. #hatandgloveorganizer #entrywayideas #winterorganization

**Image to use:** MISSLO Over-the-Door Mesh Pocket Organizer (`misslo-overdoor-mesh-pockets-1.jpg`)
