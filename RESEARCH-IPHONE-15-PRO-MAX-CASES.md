## Caveats to put in an article

- OtterBox listings were being sold by third-party resellers at check time, not OtterBox or Amazon.
  OtterBox is a commonly counterfeited brand, so either point to OtterBox's own storefront listing or flag
  it in the note.
- Drop-test claims ("4X military", "18 ft") are the manufacturers' own and haven't been independently
  verified.
- Wallet cases with cards in the back usually block wireless and MagSafe charging. Smartish says so outright.

## Suggested article angle

"iPhone 15 Pro Max Cases, Sorted by How You Use Your Phone": everyday slim, clear, MagSafe accessories user,
rugged/outdoor, wallet replacement. A short section explains the MagSafe gotcha: a case without magnets
weakens MagSafe chargers and wallets, and a card-wallet case blocks wireless charging.
