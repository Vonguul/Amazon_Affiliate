A home office is the one place where spending more is easy to justify: you sit there for hours, five days a week. The upgrades below are the ones that change how a long day feels, and they are the biggest items on the list, so they are worth getting right.

## Start with the chair

The chair is the upgrade with the most effect. Consumer Reports' ergonomics expert makes a point worth repeating: a chair marketed as ergonomic doesn't mean much on its own, and adjustability is what to look for, because one size does not fit everyone. Both chairs here are chosen for the range of adjustment they offer.

The Steelcase Series 1 has the longer track record and the more established maker. The Branch Pro has the more adjustment points on paper, with a shorter review history. If you can, sit in one before buying, or make sure you are comfortable with the return window.

## A standing desk only works if you use it

An electric standing desk is a good idea for people who will actually change position through the day. The FlexiSpot here has the strongest rating of anything in this guide and a large surface for more than one monitor. Pair it with an anti-fatigue mat, because standing on a hard floor for long periods is its own discomfort.

## A monitor arm fixes posture and frees the desk

A monitor on its own stand is rarely at the right height. An arm lets you set the screen at eye level and then move it out of the way, which also clears desk space. The Ergotron LX has a very large review base. Check the weight limit and mount pattern for your monitor.

## Buy in this order

If you can't do everything at once, the chair comes first, then the monitor arm, then a standing desk. The mat only matters once you have the desk.

## Worth being direct about

These are significant purchases and the return process matters. Check the seller on the listing: Amazon sells some, and the maker sells others. Ratings are a snapshot, and heavy items like desks can arrive damaged. Several well-known chair brands, such as Herman Miller's Aeron, appear on Amazon mostly through third-party resellers with weaker ratings, so we've left those out and chosen listings that are sold by Amazon or the maker.
