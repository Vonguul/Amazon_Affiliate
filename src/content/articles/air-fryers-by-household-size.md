An air fryer is a small convection oven with a basket, and the most important choice is size. Too small and you cook in batches, so the first batch is cold by the time the second is done. Too big and it takes over the counter for food a smaller machine would cook just as well.

## Size by the people you cook for

A rough guide: 2 to 3 quarts for one person, 4 to 5 quarts for two or three, around 6 quarts for a family of four, and a dual-basket 8 quarts or more if you want to cook a main and a side at once. Food crisps when air can move around it, so a basket filled in a single layer cooks better than a large one piled high.

## Give it a home on the counter

Air fryers need space around them: the hot air vents out of the back or side, so leave the clearance the manual specifies and don't push one under a low cabinet while it runs. If counter space is tight, a small model can live in a cabinet and come out when needed. ## Worth being direct about

Air fryer marketing often calls the food "healthier". What an air fryer does is cook with hot air and little oil, and how healthy a meal is depends on what you put in it.
