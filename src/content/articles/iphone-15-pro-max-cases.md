The iPhone 15 Pro Max is a big, heavy phone, and most cases for it fall into five groups: all-round protective, clear, slim, rugged, and wallet. Each pick below was checked on Amazon to confirm the listing is actually for the 15 Pro Max (not the similar-looking 16 Pro Max) and that it's in stock.

## First: do you use MagSafe?

This is the most common case mistake. MagSafe chargers, car mounts, stands, and stick-on wallets hold on with magnets, and they only work well through a case that has magnets built in. A case without them works with a regular wireless charger, but MagSafe accessories slide off or don't hold at all.

Every case below is marked clearly. Most have magnets. The Spigen Liquid Air and OtterBox Commuter don't, and the Smartish wallet case blocks wireless charging entirely, because the cards sit between the phone and the charger.

## All-round protection

The Spigen Tough Armor MagFit is the default recommendation: a hard shell over a shock-absorbing layer, a kickstand, and MagSafe magnets, with the highest rating of anything we checked.

## Clear

Clear cases show off the phone's color, and both clear picks here have magnets. The ESR Classic Hybrid is the most reviewed and uses an anti-yellowing coating. Clear cases still yellow over time to some degree, but coated ones hold up longer. The Spigen Ultra Hybrid MagFit is the alternative from the brand behind the top all-round pick.

## Slim

If you mostly want scratch and minor-drop protection without bulk, the Spigen Liquid Air is grippy and thin but has no magnets. The TORRAS Magnetic Slim-Fit is the slim choice for MagSafe users.

## Rugged

For work sites, outdoors, or frequent drops, the SUPCASE adds a camera-lens cover and a stand, and the Rokform adds a twist-lock for Rokform's bike and other mounts. The OtterBox Commuter is the familiar name, but note it has no MagSafe magnets and was only sold by third-party resellers when we checked.

## Wallet

The Smartish Wallet Slayer holds up to four cards plus cash and replaces a separate wallet. The trade-off is no wireless or MagSafe charging. An alternative is a magnetic case plus a separate MagSafe wallet that snaps on and off.

## Worth knowing

- **Drop-test numbers are the makers' own.** Claims like "military-grade" or "11 ft" come from the brands and aren't independently tested. Treat them as rough comparisons, not guarantees.
- **Review counts are shared across colors.** On Amazon, one case model's reviews usually cover every color it comes in.
- **Check the model.** The 15 Pro Max, 16 Pro Max, and 15 Plus look alike but aren't interchangeable: the 16 Pro Max is slightly larger and adds a Camera Control button, and the 15 Plus has a different camera layout. Make sure the listing names your exact phone.
