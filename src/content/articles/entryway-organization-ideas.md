The entryway is the one room in a home guaranteed to accumulate clutter daily, because it's the transition point for everything coming in and out — shoes, coats, keys, mail, umbrellas. Fixing it isn't about buying more storage everywhere; it's about giving each of those four categories a specific, obvious spot.

## Shoes need a landing spot, not a pile

A pile of shoes by the door happens because there's no clearly faster alternative — kicking shoes off and leaving them is always going to be quicker than carrying them somewhere else. A bench with built-in cubbies removes that friction: putting them away is exactly as fast as leaving them out, since the cubby is right there.

## Keys, coats, and mail each need their own spot

These three items scatter because they don't share a natural home — a coat goes on a chair, keys go on a counter, mail goes wherever it was set down. A single wall-mounted piece that handles all three in one spot removes the decision entirely.

## Wet umbrellas need somewhere that isn't the floor

An umbrella stand with a drip tray is a small, cheap fix for a recurring problem: a wet umbrella leaning in a corner leaves a puddle every single time it rains, and nobody remembers to put a towel down for it.

## Wet or muddy shoes need a surface that can actually be cleaned

A boot tray under the bench catches the water and mud that shoes and boots inevitably bring in, and unlike a fabric mat, it can be hosed off outside instead of needing to be laundered.
