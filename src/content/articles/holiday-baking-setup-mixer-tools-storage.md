Holiday baking is the one time of year many kitchens run at full volume: several batches, several kinds of cookies, maybe bread, all in a few days. The right tools make that faster. Where they're kept decides whether you enjoy it.

## The stand mixer question

A stand mixer earns its counter space if you bake regularly or make big batches. It mixes while your hands are free for the next step, and it handles stiff doughs that wear out a hand mixer. If you bake a few times a year, a good hand mixer does most of the same work and fits in a drawer. Be honest about which you are before buying the big one.

If you do buy one, the KitchenAid tilt-head models are the default for a reason: they've been made in much the same form for decades, and parts and attachments are easy to find.

## Make the mixer easy to reach

A stand mixer weighs over 20 pounds, and that's the main reason they end up pushed into a back corner and forgotten. A slider mat under it means you can pull it forward to use and push it back with one hand. A fitted cover keeps it clean between uses and gives the beaters and shield a home. This is the organizing step most people skip, and it decides whether an expensive mixer gets used.

## The tools that make batches consistent

A scale, a set of scoops and a proper half sheet pan do more for consistent cookies than any recipe. Weighing ingredients removes the biggest variable in baking. Scooping makes every cookie the same size. A heavy, plain aluminum pan heats evenly. The cooling racks fit inside the pan, so the two store together. For keeping the rest of your baking supplies in order, see [baking supplies and bakeware organization](/articles/baking-supplies-bakeware-organization-ideas/).

## Worth being direct about

A stand mixer is a big purchase that many people use a handful of times a year. If you're not sure, start with the hand mixer and the scale. Mixer prices on Amazon swing widely by color and over the season, so compare a few colors before buying. Check that any cover or slider mat matches your mixer's size and style: tilt-head and bowl-lift models are different shapes.
