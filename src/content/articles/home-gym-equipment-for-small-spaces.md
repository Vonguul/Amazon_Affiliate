In a room that's also a living room, a home office or a spare bedroom, the best equipment is the kind that disappears when you're done. That rules out most of what fills a commercial gym, and it points to three things: adjustable dumbbells, a bench that folds, and, if you want cardio, something that rolls away.

This guide is about the equipment. For where it lives afterwards, see our [home gym organization ideas](/articles/home-gym-organization-ideas/).

## Adjustable dumbbells replace a whole rack

A set of fixed dumbbells from 5 to 50 lb is ten pairs and a rack the size of a bookcase. One pair of adjustable dumbbells covers the same range in the space of a shoebox each. That's the single biggest space saving in a home gym.

The PowerBlock is the most compact and changes weight with a pin. The NordicTrack uses a dial and comes with trays that show each weight, which some people find easier to read. The Amazon Basics set is the low-cost start: slower to change, but fine for a few exercises at fixed weights.

## A bench that folds

A bench turns dumbbells into a full routine: presses, rows, step-ups, seated work. Both benches here adjust from decline to upright and fold. The FLYBIRD is the most-reviewed and has more angle settings; the YOLEO folds smaller, arrives ready to use and fits under a bed.

## Cardio: a walking pad or a real treadmill

A walking pad is a low-profile belt with no rails, made for walking pace, and it slides under a sofa or bed. It suits walking while you work at a standing desk. The UREVO has a running mode up to 6.2 mph, but if you run often, a full treadmill is the better tool.

If you want to run, the Sunny treadmill is a full machine with a wider belt, a stronger motor and auto incline, and it still folds. Check ceiling height before buying any treadmill: you'll stand several inches higher on the deck, and incline lifts the front further.

## Worth being direct about

Equipment doesn't make a routine. If you're starting out, a pair of adjustable dumbbells and a bench cover almost everything, and the rest can wait until you're using those three times a week. Weight capacities and dimensions are the makers' figures, so check them against your space and your body weight. Treadmills and benches are heavy to return, so measure first. If you have a health condition, talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program.
