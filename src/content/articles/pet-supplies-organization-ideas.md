Pet supplies rarely start out as clutter — they start as a bag of food, a handful of toys, and a leash by the door, and multiply from there without ever getting an actual home. Food bags get propped in corners, toys end up scattered across every room, and leashes disappear into whatever pile is closest to the door. The four fixes below give pet gear real structure instead of letting it spread across the house.

## Keep food sealed and easy to move

A bag of food left open in a pantry loses freshness quickly and is an awkward, heavy shape to carry or store. A wheeled airtight container seals out air and pests while staying easy to roll to wherever it's needed, instead of sitting propped open in a corner.

## Give toys one place to land

Toys scattered across the floor are one of the fastest ways a living room stops looking like a living room. A single collapsible basket with sturdy handles gives every toy a defined spot to return to, and it folds flat when it's not in use.

## Stop leashes and collars from living on the floor

Leashes, collars, and poop bags dropped by the door on the way in usually just stay wherever they land. A wall-mounted rack with hooks and a shelf gives each item a fixed spot, so grabbing the leash on the way out doesn't mean searching for it first.

## Keep treats fresh instead of clipped shut in a bag

A half-open treat bag closed with a chip clip goes stale quickly and doesn't stack cleanly with anything else on the counter. A set of matching airtight jars keeps treats fresh longer and lets different types stay visually separated instead of mixed together in one bag.
