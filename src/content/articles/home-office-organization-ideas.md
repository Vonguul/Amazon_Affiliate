A home office desk accumulates clutter faster than almost any other surface in a house, because it's the landing spot for cables, paper, and loose supplies every single day. The fix isn't a bigger desk — it's giving each category of clutter a specific place that isn't "wherever there's room."

## Cables first — they're the most visible clutter

A tangle of cords and a loose power strip under the desk is often the single most visible sign of disorganization in a home office, even when everything else is tidy. A dedicated cable tray mounted under the desk clears that up in one step.

## Reclaim the dead space under the monitor

The gap between the desk surface and the bottom of a raised monitor is usually empty space doing nothing. A monitor stand with a built-in drawer uses that exact gap for supplies that would otherwise clutter the desk surface.

## Give paper a system instead of a pile

Paper piles grow because there's no clear separation between "just arrived," "still need to deal with," and "ready to file." Stacked trays create that separation physically, which does most of the organizational work on their own.

## Drawers and floor storage last

Once the desk surface itself is under control, a proper drawer organizer stops the junk-drawer problem, and a rolling file cart gives overflow documents a real home — off the floor, but out of the way until actually needed.
