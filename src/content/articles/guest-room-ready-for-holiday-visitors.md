A spare room, a home office or a corner of the basement becomes a guest room with surprisingly little: a decent bed, somewhere for the suitcase, towels that are theirs, and a way to charge a phone. You can set all of it up in an afternoon, and all of it puts away again when the visit is over.

## The bed decides everything

You have two realistic choices for a room that isn't a bedroom the rest of the year. A raised air mattress sleeps two on a queen footprint and packs into a bag. A folding bed takes more closet space, but there's no inflating and nothing to leak.

For couples, the air mattress is the practical choice. For a single adult or a child, especially for a longer stay, the folding bed avoids the first-night problem of air mattresses: the material stretches as it settles, so it feels softer in the morning. Inflate it the day before, then top it up before bedtime.

## Then make it a real bed

What makes a guest bed comfortable is mostly what goes on top of it. A topper hides the air-mattress feel and keeps the sheets from sliding. A set of sheets and a comforter that belong to the guest room mean nothing has to be borrowed from your own bed. Keep them together in one bag or bin in the linen closet so the next visit is a ten-minute setup.

## Get the suitcase off the floor

A suitcase opened on the floor takes over the room within a day. A luggage rack keeps it at waist height and stops it spilling across the floor. It's a small purchase, and it's what makes a spare room feel like it was set up for someone.

## Towels and a place to charge

Guests hate asking where the towels are. A set that belongs to the guest room, folded on the end of the bed, answers the question before it's asked. A charging station on the nightstand answers the next question. If your linen closet needs sorting before visitors arrive, see [linen closet organization ideas](/articles/linen-closet-organization-ideas/).

## Worth being direct about

An air mattress, even a good one, isn't a real bed for a long stay. Reviews of every model mention the occasional unit that loses air, so test yours a few days before guests arrive, while there's still time to return it. The folding bed's cot width suits one sleeper only. Check the dimensions of anything you buy against the room, especially with a door that has to open past the bed.
