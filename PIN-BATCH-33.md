## Pin 1

**Title:** Original Game Boxes Are the Wrong Shape for a Shelf

**Description:** Every board game ships in a box sized and shaped a little differently, with corners that crush the first time something heavy stacks on top. Uniform slim cases fix the stacking problem and free up real shelf space. #boardgamestorage #gameroomorganization #puzzlestorage

**Image to use:** IRIS USA Slim Board Game Storage Case (`iris-boardgame-storage-1.jpg`)

## Pin 2

**Title:** A Puzzle in Progress Shouldn't Hold Your Table Hostage

**Description:** A puzzle mid-assembly means the table's out of commission for days, sometimes weeks. A roll-up mat with sorting trays included frees the table without losing progress. #puzzlestorage #jigsawpuzzle #howtoorganizepuzzles

**Image to use:** Newverest Jigsaw Puzzle Roll-Up Mat (`newverest-puzzle-mat-1.jpg`)

## Pin 3

**Title:** Pull-Out Drawers Beat a Shelf Once the Collection Gets Big

**Description:** For a genuinely large board game collection, individual pull-out drawers mean finding one specific game doesn't mean pulling every box off a shelf to check what's behind it. #boardgamestorage #gameroomorganization #playroomstorage

**Image to use:** Board Game Storage Cabinet with Pull-Out Drawers (`boardgame-storage-cabinet-1.jpg`)
